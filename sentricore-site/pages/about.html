About Sentricore

We defend the first packet of every connection: the DNS lookup.

The story

Sentricore started as one maintainer's Raspberry Pi running a weekend Python script that answered 0.0.0.0 for malware domains seen in the news. It blocked so much junk that it became the household resolver overnight — and then the requests started: caching, rate limits, a dashboard the family could look at, metrics that Grafana could scrape.

Each feature was pulled into the open, tested, and shipped. Today Sentricore DNS is a small, boring, dependable stack — dnslib, Flask, SQLite — that does what enterprise resolvers do, on hardware you already own, with data nobody else can see.

What we believe

  • Privacy is architecture. If your resolver isn't on your network, your browsing history is someone else's product.
  • Boring beats clever. One process, plain files, no microservices. You should be able to read every line that touches your traffic.
  • Security tools must be observable. A block that reports nothing is a rumor. Everything gets logged, counted, and exported.
  • Open source is the trust layer. MIT license, public CI, reproducible containers.
