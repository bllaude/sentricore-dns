Short posts from inside the proxy: what we shipped, what we measured, and what broke.

2026-09-14

Why we sinkhole to 0.0.0.0 instead of dropping

Dropping a blocked query makes browsers hang for seconds on timeouts. A fast, authoritative NXDOMAIN-style answer at 0.0.0.0 with a short TTL gives users an instant "this is blocked" signal without leaking anything upstream. We walk through the UX traces that convinced us.

2026-08-30

Batched SQLite writes: 40× fewer fsyncs at 1k QPS

Per-query inserts melted our SD cards. Buffering events and flushing once per second kept durability where it mattered (audit history) while making sustained throughput a non-issue. Numbers, flamegraphs, and the flush-interval tuning guide are inside.

2026-08-11

Anatomy of a DNS amplification attack — and the token bucket that stops it

A compromised IoT bulb can turn an open resolver into a cannon. We replay a captured amplification attempt against Sentricore's per-client rate limiter and show exactly where the traffic dies: 50 QPS sustained, 100 burst, everything else dropped before it reaches your upstream.

2026-07-22

Normalizing five blocklist formats so you only write one parser

Domain-per-line, hosts files, ad-block syntax, comments, IDN edge cases — real feeds are messy. Here's the loader that merges files and URLs into one set, hot-reloads every 300 seconds, and never blocks a restart.
