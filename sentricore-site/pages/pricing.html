Frequently asked questions

Is anything actually paywalled?
No. Every capability in the capability matrix ships in the MIT-licensed repository. Paid tiers buy maintainer time, not software features.
Why would I pay for support on free software?
Same reason people pay for RHEL: someone accountable answers the ticket at 2 a.m., reviews your config, and keeps a private mirror of hardened blocklist feeds.
Do you host a cloud version?
Deliberately not. A filtering resolver that sees all your queries should live on hardware you control — that's the product thesis.
Can we fork and self-support?
Absolutely. MIT license, no CLA, attribution-only. Fork away.
What are the resource requirements?
A Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W comfortably proxies a household. Budget ~50 MB RAM plus room for your SQLite history file.
